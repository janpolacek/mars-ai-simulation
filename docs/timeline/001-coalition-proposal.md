# 001 — Coalition proposal

## Private situation

Research leaders identify a gap between short Mars demonstrations and sustained,
openly documented surface science. They begin informal conversations about a joint
robotic programme that combines geology, local environmental sensing, and durable
rover operations. No mission is funded or technically frozen yet.

## Constraints and decisions

- The group wants a visibly multinational programme without implying that real
  agencies have committed money or endorsed the fictional mission.
- The first mission must be narrow enough to be credible: one rover, one landing
  site, and a science package that earns later expansion.
- Partner names, governance body, target launch window, and budget remain open.

## Allowed public angle

The first public Red Horizon story may say that a group is exploring a collaborative
Mars mission and explain the intended scientific/engineering focus. It must use
future-facing language—"is being studied" or "is being proposed"—not claim a
confirmed launch, selected rover, or committed partners.

## Spoilers and handoff

Do not mention selected instruments, RH-01, launch vehicle, landing region, or a
known operational issue. A useful visual is a restrained abstract identity or
planning-room motif, not a finished spacecraft render.
