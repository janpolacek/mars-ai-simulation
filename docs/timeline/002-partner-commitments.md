# 002 — Partner commitments

## Private situation

The proposal becomes a named programme. Partner groups agree to share a mission
architecture, science access, and public communications. They divide broad
responsibilities among spacecraft systems, instruments, ground operations, and
data stewardship while retaining independent technical review.

## Constraints and decisions

- Create fictional coalition/member names before public publication if real agency
  roles would imply endorsement.
- The programme's governance must allow candid reporting of risk and anomalies.
- Exact funding numbers and contractual details are unnecessary unless the human
  explicitly chooses to make them part of the fiction.

## Allowed public angle

Announce the Red Horizon programme, its shared scientific purpose, and the idea of
partner contributions. Explain that technical selections are forthcoming. The
release may establish recurring organisational names but cannot name the final
payload or hardware.

## Spoilers and handoff

Do not reveal which contributor owns the signature sensor or rover subsystems.
Candidate media: programme mark, partner-map graphic, or a simple technical
planning visual. Dependency: 001 must be public first.
