# 006 — Cruise

## Private situation

The spacecraft spends months en route to Mars. Navigation corrections, power
management, communications checks, and instrument safing are routine. A small,
manageable issue may emerge—such as a sensor calibration drift, thermal margin, or
communications schedule constraint—to establish the team's repair discipline.

## Constraints and decisions

- Select at most one meaningful issue and ensure it does not contradict safe
  landing or the later rover condition.
- Explain normal cruise in approachable terms; routine is a valid story when it
  teaches readers how missions operate.
- Keep the issue's root cause and mitigation technically modest unless researched.

## Allowed public angle

Publish periodic short mission updates: successful checkout, a planned correction,
or a cautious explanation of a manageable technical concern and the next review.

## Spoilers and handoff

Do not name the final landing site, claim entry readiness before step 007, or imply
a surface discovery. Dependency: 005.
