# 003 — Vehicle design freeze

## Private situation

RH-01 Pathfinder reaches a stable, testable configuration. The rover carries the
approved payload on a compact six-wheel platform with a central camera mast and a
single front contact arm. Its appearance is fixed in the vehicle dossier before
any visual is treated as canonical.

## Constraints and decisions

- The canonical reference package defines silhouette, colour placement, mast,
  wheels, suspension, arm, and rear panel; later images must preserve them.
- Any design trade-off should be practical: mass, dust protection, power, thermal
  cycling, communications, or terrain handling.
- "Design freeze" does not imply the vehicle is built or launch-ready.

## Allowed public angle

Release the first rover dossier and approved studio references. Explain the
design's science role and limitations, avoiding unverified performance statistics.

## Spoilers and handoff

No launch announcement, landing site, surface photography, damage, or discovery.
The visual creator must produce and the human approve canonical references before
editorial scene images. Dependency: 002.
