# 002 — Payload selection

## Private situation

Engineering and science teams select a compact payload for RH-01: panoramic and
navigation imaging, weather measurement, mineral/rock sensing, and a contact tool
for close inspection. A European-led sensor becomes a visible programme
contribution, but its exact name and technique remain open.

## Constraints and decisions

- Every instrument must have a visible or explainable physical home on the rover.
- Claims stay within ordinary robotic planetary science: mapping, mineral context,
  environmental monitoring, and careful sampling—not direct proof of life.
- Instrument names should be pronounceable and distinct once approved.

## Allowed public angle

Publish a payload-selection update that explains the questions the rover will ask
and why a multi-sensor package matters. A future wiki page may define each approved
instrument in plain language.

## Spoilers and handoff

Do not publish a final rover render unless step 003 is approved. Do not promise
the landing site, launch vehicle, or discovery. Candidate media: labelled but
non-final instrument concept, or a sensor-focused editorial illustration.
