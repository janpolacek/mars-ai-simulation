# 008 — Landing

## Private situation

Red Horizon reaches a scientifically useful, visually distinctive terrain. The
landing succeeds and RH-01 is alive, but data suggests a minor condition that may
limit a later operation: for example a dust-related calibration concern, actuator
margin, or intermittent thermal/communications behaviour. It is not a catastrophe.

## Constraints and decisions

- Confirm basic power, communications, and rover survival before reporting a
  successful landing.
- Decide the exact condition only after it is technically reviewed and consistent
  with steps 009–011.
- Avoid false drama; an early uncertainty can be public without a premature cause.

## Allowed public angle

Publish a landing confirmation with what is known, what remains to be checked,
and the first expected surface operations. A landscape/lander visual must be
approved and not depict unreleased rover actions.

## Spoilers and handoff

Do not claim all systems are perfect, publish the first rover POV, or disclose the
final repair plan. Dependency: 007.
