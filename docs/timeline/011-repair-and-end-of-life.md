# 011 — Repair and end of life

## Private situation

The operations team attempts a cautious mitigation for the persistent condition.
It extends useful work or safely changes the rover's operating envelope rather
than restoring it to an impossible perfect state. Eventually power, thermal,
communications, mechanical wear, or another approved limit ends regular contact.
The mission closes transparently with its scientific and operational record intact.

## Constraints and decisions

- Select the mitigation, remaining capability, final failure mode, and final
  message only after reviewing every preceding public fact.
- End of life is dignified and technically coherent: no melodramatic destruction,
  unexplained rescue, or sudden miraculous breakthrough.
- Preserve a distinction between a final contact, an official end declaration,
  and later archival/wiki updates.

## Allowed public angle

Publish a repair/update report, a final status when appropriate, and a retrospective
that states what RH-01 achieved, what remained uncertain, and what its data means
for future missions.

## Spoilers and handoff

This is the only step allowed to close the mission. Before release, the human story
owner approves the final sequence, public wording, authoritative final media, and
archival strategy. Dependency: 010.
