# 011 — Operations and discovery

## Private situation

RH-01 begins measured traverses and layered observations. It examines rocks,
maps route conditions, and builds evidence for a scientifically meaningful but
grounded finding—such as a mineral pattern, sedimentary history, or altered-rock
environment. Equipment wear and the earlier condition create operational limits.

## Constraints and decisions

- The discovery must emerge in stages: observation, corroboration, cautious
  interpretation, and a stated limitation. It is not evidence of extant life.
- Routine operations have narrative value: wheel routes, scan targets, weather,
  power, and careful choices make the mission feel alive.
- Establish only operational degradation that the later mitigation/end-of-life
  step can credibly address.

## Allowed public angle

Release regular, modest mission updates and one deeper science explainer when the
evidence is mature. Wiki pages can provide enduring context for approved terrain,
instruments, and findings.

## Spoilers and handoff

Do not claim a full repair, terminal failure, or mission conclusion. Dependency:
010. Continuity review is required for every new observation and scene image.
