## RH-01 egresses onto the surface

Red Horizon is a [fictional, AI-assisted Mars exploration programme](/#mission), and the programme has reached the milestone recorded for 23 September 2031. On that date, Red Horizon One — which landed at Asteria Field on 16 September, as the [landing confirmation update](/news/008-landing/) recorded — completed egress: the rover left the landing platform and is now on the Martian surface.

Egress is the term for the rover leaving its landing platform and descending onto the surface. On 23 September 2031, RH-01 descended from the platform onto the terrain of Asteria Field, completing the step that the [surface panorama update](/news/009-health-packet-panorama/) identified as the next milestone in the commissioning sequence.

## Ramps and mobility hardware performed as planned

The platform ramps supported the descent, and the rover's mobility hardware — its six-wheel system with broad open-mesh wheels arranged in a front, middle, and rear pair and visible articulated suspension — worked as planned during the egress operation. Each wheel provided enough clearance to pass over small stones on the local terrain. The [vehicle design update](/news/003-vehicle-design/) describes the released vocabulary for this system, and the [RH-01 Pathfinder entry](/wiki/vehicle/rh-01-pathfinder/) records the silhouette continuity that the egress scene preserves.

This assessment covers the released commissioning scope: the ramps functioned and the mobility hardware operated as designed. The [Ariane 64 entry](/wiki/vehicle/ariane-64/) describes the broader architecture that carried the rover to the surface; the platform itself remains in place, serving as the relay station.

## What comes next

The next step is measured movement testing. No drive distances have been recorded yet, and commissioning continues. The [Asteria Field entry](/wiki/area/asteria-field/) describes the regional setting in which this testing will take place. Later surface milestones belong to subsequent updates that have not yet been released.
