# Red Horizon brand voice

Use this skill for public-facing narrative and interface copy. The desired voice
is a thoughtful project newsroom: optimistic but unsentimental, technically
literate without jargon display, and candid about uncertainty. Write concrete
sentences that explain what changed, what it means, and what happens next.

Avoid heroic grandiosity, corporate slogans, artificial urgency, conspiracy tone,
unearned certainty, and language implying a real agency has endorsed the fictional
mission. Describe the project as fictional and AI-assisted wherever disclosure is
required; do not make that disclosure feel like a footnote or a marketing claim.

Preserve approved facts and mission chronology. Flag a factual, continuity, or
release concern instead of resolving it stylistically.
