## Canonical sources

### Real-world references

#### 1. ESA — ExoMars Rosalind Franklin rover instrument suite

- **URL**: https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Human_and_Robotic_Exploration/Exploration/ExoMars/All_instruments_onboard_Rosalind_Franklin_rover
- **Publisher**: European Space Agency
- **Published**: 20 August 2019 (accessed September 2026)
- **Content**: Rosalind Franklin rover carries eight instruments (Pasteur payload): PanCam (panoramic stereo cameras + high-resolution camera), Enfys (IR spectrometer), CLUPI (close-up imager), WISDOM (ground-penetrating radar), ADRON (neutron detector), MicrOmega (hyperspectral microscope), MOMA (organic molecule analyser), RLS (Raman spectrometer). PanCam provides geological context, stereo 3D, and multispectral imaging.
- **Relevance**: Establishes real-world precedent for multi-sensor European Mars rover payloads combining panoramic imaging, IR spectroscopy, and contact inspection.

#### 2. ESA Exploration Science — PanCam description

- **URL**: https://explorationscience.esa.int/project/exomars-rosalind-franklin-rfm/
- **Publisher**: European Space Agency
- **Content**: PanCam includes stereo Wide Angle Cameras (WACs) with 11-position filter wheels and a High Resolution Camera (HRC). Used for geological context, target selection, and 3D vision. Enfys IR spectrometer works with PanCam for mineral composition.
- **Relevance**: Real instrument example for panoramic and multispectral imaging categories.

#### 3. NASA — Mars 2020 Perseverance Science Instruments

- **URL**: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/science-instruments/
- **Publisher**: NASA
- **Published**: ongoing (accessed September 2026)
- **Content**: Seven instruments: MastCam-Z (zoom mast cameras, stereo + multispectral), SuperCam (LIBS, Raman, VISIR spectroscopy + RMI + microphone), PIXL (X-ray fluorescence + micro-imager), SHERLOC (Raman + luminescence + WATSON close-up camera), MEDA (weather station), RIMFAX (ground-penetrating radar), MOXIE (oxygen ISRU). MEDA measures wind, temperature, humidity, and dust. SuperCam built with French (CNES/IRAP) contribution.
- **Relevance**: Establishes real-world precedent for all four instrument categories (panoramic imaging, mineral/rock sensing, weather, contact tool).

#### 4. CNES — SuperCam (Franco-American instrument on Perseverance)

- **URL**: https://cnes.fr/en/projects/supercam
- **Publisher**: Centre National d'Etudes Spatiales (France)
- **Content**: SuperCam mast unit built by French consortium (IRAP, CNES, CNRS laboratories). Instrument suite combines LIBS, Raman, VISIR, RMI camera, and microphone. France-led contribution is a visible international partnership element on Perseverance.
- **Relevance**: Real-world precedent for a European-led sensor contribution on a Mars rover.

#### 5. Springer — Mars 2020 Mission Overview (Space Science Reviews, 2020)

- **URL**: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11214-020-00762-y
- **Authors**: Mustard et al.
- **Content**: Overview of Perseverance science objectives and payload. Instruments chosen to map mineralogy, detect organics, measure weather, and enable sample return. Confirms standard categories: imaging, spectroscopy, weather, contact tools.
- **Relevance**: Peer-reviewed confirmation of standard Mars rover payload categories.

#### 6. PMC — The PanCam Instrument for the ExoMars Rover

- **URL**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5568594/
- **Authors**: Coates et al.
- **Content**: Detailed PanCam instrument description. Wide Angle Cameras with filter wheels for multispectral geology and atmospheric science; HRC for close-up rock texture. PanCam establishes geological and morphological context for rover mission.
- **Relevance**: Detailed technical reference for panoramic and contact imaging categories.

#### 7. ESA EPSC — PanCam: the 'science eyes' of Rosalind Franklin (2024)

- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.5194/epsc2024-927
- **Content**: PanCam operational objectives and design for the 2028 mission. Stereo pair, filter wheels, HRC, 3D vision. Confirms role in geological context and target selection.
- **Relevance**: Current (2024) confirmation of panoramic imaging instrument category.
